Trying to become good at software engineering is an overwhelming task.
My old drum teacher had an analogy for learning to play the drums; your skill is like a rope full of knots. Whenever you learn something it's like untying one of those knots. The 100% technically accomplished drummer would not have any knots on their rope left, it would just be an entirely straight one. This is a pretty accurate analogy for certain things, e.g. learning limb independence, being able to play different rhytms independently with each limb. I find it useful for my technical learning too.

- I've found it especially helpful in terms of learning a particular thing or area. If you can identify the knots involved in that thing, you can go ahead and loosen them one at a time. At the end of it you should hopefully land where you want (link to Martin's blog post on side projects).
- A knot worked only half-loose might tighten itself again.
